Our Story

A little piece of Europe at Sakinaka Junction.

Café Crema began with one idea: Andheri deserved a café that feels like a long lunch in Lisbon, any time of day. We source single-estate Indian beans, ferment our pizza dough for 48 hours, and bake every dessert in-house.
